Project Process & Timeline

Phase 1 – Concept & Design (Q1–Q2 2025)

We began by researching the needs of professionals in extreme environments and identifying the limitations of conventional mice. During this phase, the concept for the Luminar Mouse was developed, including sketches, feasibility studies, and initial design ideas for the sensor cube, rugged pad, and boltable system.

Phase 2 – Prototyping & Testing (Q3 2025)

The first prototypes were built and tested on curved, slanted, and hostile surfaces. Rigorous stress tests evaluated heat, water, and blast resistance, while user feedback from industrial and military trials guided iterative improvements. This ensured that the design was not only innovative but also durable and reliable.

Phase 3 – Production & Launch (Q4 2025–Q1 2026)

With the final design approved, small-scale production was initiated and certifications obtained. The Luminar Mouse was launched with targeted marketing campaigns emphasizing control, resilience, and precision, making it available to industrial, military, and extreme-environment users.
